# Handy 2 2.4.0

## New

- **Eye gaze** ("Move the cursor with: Eyes"): the cursor follows where you look. Calibrate once with the nine-point calibration (click "Calibrate gaze" in the Control tab). The calibration runs in fullscreen; Cancel stops it. Expect roughly a third of the screen in accuracy.
- **Pupils only** ("Pupils (not precise)"): the cursor follows the position of the pupils and ignores the angle of the head. It uses the same calibration as Eye gaze. Keep the head still.

## Notes

- Gaze works only while the eyes are open and the head is turned less than 35 degrees.
- Calibrate again after moving the chair or the camera.
